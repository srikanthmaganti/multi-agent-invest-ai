These documents are reference material, not a complete or necessarily
up-to-date representation of FCA rules. Preserve each document's title,
publication/version date, source URL, and download date as metadata when
building the retrieval index. Use retrieved passages with citations and have
qualified compliance reviewers validate the corpus and resulting answers.
